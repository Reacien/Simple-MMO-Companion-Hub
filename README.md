# Simple MMO Companion Hub

A Windows desktop app for SimpleMMO players. Play the game with up to three accounts in one place, with your cooldowns, travel stats, bounties and reminders beside the game.

**Unofficial — not affiliated with SimpleMMO or its developers.**

## Download

**[Download Simple-MMO-Companion-Hub-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Reacien/Simple-MMO-Companion-Hub/raw/main/Simple-MMO-Companion-Hub-Setup.exe)**

Run it, and it downloads and installs the newest version. After that the app keeps itself up to date: when there's a new version, it shows you what changed and asks before updating.

Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"** or **"unknown publisher"**, because the setup isn't code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a SimpleMMO account and its API key.

Prefer the full installer? Every [release](../../releases/latest) has `Simple-MMO-Companion-Hub-Installer.exe`, with that version's notes.

## What it does

- **Web client:** the game in tabs, with a Companion panel beside it (walk stats, timers, players on the page, guild requirements). Fights open in a popup so your page stays put.
- **Mobile client:** the game in a phone-sized window, with the same Companion panel.
- **Chat client** and a **Calculator**.
- **Game Database:** items, enemies, your stats, and what's best for your level.
- **Timers and reminders:** energy, quest points, dailies, weekly and monthly resets, world boss and more, with Windows notifications.
- **Bounties:** a bounty watch with low-HP alerts, rows coloured by your chance to win, and an auto-reload you start, pause and stop.
- **Win chance** and HP for players on a page and on player profiles.

It **only reads the game and alerts you**. It never clicks, steps, fights or submits anything for you.

## Privacy

Your data stays on your PC. Your API key is stored encrypted by Windows and only sent to SimpleMMO's own servers. SimpleMMO says using your key in third-party tools is at your own risk.
